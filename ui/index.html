Agentic studio console — faceless video, end to end

Turn a single topic into a narrated faceless video, with every production step in view.

ReelDirector runs the line in order: an agent writes the script, scenes are planned from those exact lines, then voice, assembly, and QC pass the job forward. Nothing is hidden behind a spinner — the stages and log show what happened, or what failed.
