## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one finished cinematic concept illustration, landscape 3:2 composition, for an AI filmmaking studio's empty preview. An imaginative sculptural river gorge at blue hour: layered sapphire and indigo mountains, a gently luminous winding blue river through massive warm sandstone forms, and a tiny observatory-like structure on a distant ledge for scale. Rich tactile paper-cut and carefully crafted miniature-set art, sophisticated editorial film still, refined atmospheric lighting, painterly materials and realistic depth. Restrained navy, sapphire, muted soft peach sandstone; striking central composition, quieter darker outer edges with generous negative space near lower edge for an eventual UI overlay. The entire image is the scene, no frames, no interface. No robots, no people, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermarks. Exceptional visual polish without neon cyberpunk or generic cartoon aesthetics.
